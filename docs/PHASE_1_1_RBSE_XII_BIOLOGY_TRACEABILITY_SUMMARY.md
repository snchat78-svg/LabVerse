## 5. Scientific, ethics, safety and rights boundary

1. Quantitative ecological/environmental results require a named method, calibration, units, sampling design, provenance and uncertainty. A beaker/filter paper is not a specialist measuring instrument.
2. pH paper gives approximate indicator readings, not a calibrated pH-meter result.
3. No pathogen culture, live unknown microorganism handling, human clinical sample collection, animal capture/dissection, or unapproved field sampling.
4. Genetics and pedigree models use abstract or fictional data only; never request real family medical/genetic histories.
5. Histology, embryology and parasite labels need page-level image inspection and Biology expert review before publication.
6. Teacher must approve the project topic, method, dataset/sample source and ethics/safety boundary.
7. Original LabVerse geometry/text or licensed imagery is required; source photos/NCERT artwork are not automatically reusable.
8. No model/3D asset was implemented and no scientific/software tests were run. All IDs are proposals/plans only.
