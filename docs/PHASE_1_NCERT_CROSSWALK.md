# Phase 1 — NCERT/CIET Grade 11–12 Practical Crosswalk

Audit date: 2026-10-31  
Source status: Official NCERT-CIET activity pages were opened and their indexed rows were inspected. This is a source crosswalk, not yet a complete RBSE syllabus match or a teacher-approved practical list.

## Official catalogue pages

## Grade 11 — Physics

CIET lists laboratory-manual experiments including:
- Vernier callipers; screw gauge; spherometer; beam balance.
- Parallelogram law of vector addition.
- Simple pendulum L–T and L–T² graphs.
- Limiting friction and normal reaction.
- Force versus sin(theta) for an inclined plane.
- Young's modulus with Searle's apparatus.
- Helical-spring force constant and effective mass.
- Boyle's law.
- Surface tension by capillary rise.
- Viscosity from terminal velocity.
- Cooling curve.
- Sonometer frequency/length/tension relation.
- Velocity of sound using resonance tube.
- Specific heat capacity by method of mixtures.

Additional listed items include inclined-track motion, capillary rise, atmospheric pressure, Bernoulli's theorem, coupled-pendulum resonance, wave reflection/transmission, standing waves and projectile motion.

## NCERT manual text and asset/inventory implications

NCERT Class XI Physics manual (2025–26 online edition):
https://ncert.gov.in/pdf/publication/sciencelaboratorymanuals/classXI/physics/kelm101.pdf

The manual explicitly includes experiments, activities, projects and demonstrations, and describes apparatus/materials, principle, procedure, observations, calculations, result, precautions and sources of error. It also describes experimental errors, graphing, significant figures, instrument limitations, repeated measurements and further investigation. This aligns with LabVerse's broader research workflow.

NCERT Class XI Chemistry manual (2025–26 online edition):
https://ncert.gov.in/pdf/publication/sciencelaboratorymanuals/classXI/chemistry/kelm201.pdf

Its contents include basic equipment/procedures, handling reagent bottles, heating devices, glass-tube cutting/bending/drawing, cork boring, heating, filtration, titrimetric analysis and systematic qualitative analysis. This informs the initial apparatus taxonomy.

NCERT Class XI Biology manual (2025–26 online edition):
https://ncert.gov.in/pdf/publication/sciencelaboratorymanuals/classXI/biology/kelm301.pdf

Its introductory equipment section identifies a dissection box with scissors, scalpels, forceps, dissecting needles, razor, hand lens, dropper and fine brush, alongside lab record, coat/apron and towel. Safety and supervision must accompany sharp instruments and reagents.

The NCERT manuals display all-rights-reserved notices. LabVerse must create original explanations/illustrations/3D assets or obtain permission/licences; it must not copy entire manual pages or figures into the app.
