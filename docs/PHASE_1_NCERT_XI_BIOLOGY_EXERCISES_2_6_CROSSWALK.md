## 3. Critical mismatch and handling rule

The NCERT manual contents page identifies mitosis as Exercise 6 (printed p.39), while the NCERT-CIET Grade 11 activity index displays "Study of mitosis" under Exercise 1. This is a real source-index mismatch.

LabVerse must:
1. retain the manual exercise identity NCERT-XI-BIO-EX-06;
2. retain the CIET raw row and DIKSHA content ID as a separate external resource record;
3. add a discrepancy relation between the two records;
4. avoid renumbering the manual exercise or treating the DIKSHA metadata field as a correction;
5. resolve only after inspecting the full DIKSHA metadata/content and the complete manual body.

Exercise 2 and Exercise 3 have matching topic/number labels in the two indexes, but their linked resource bodies were not extracted; title agreement alone is not procedure validation.

## 5. Rights, safety and content reuse

- The NCERT Class XI manual states ALL RIGHTS RESERVED. Cite and map it, but create original LabVerse explanations and models; do not copy NCERT prose, diagrams, tables or illustrations without permission.
- DIKSHA's official FAQ lists permitted content licence families, including CC BY, CC BY-SA, CC BY-NC, CC BY-NC-SA, CC BY-ND and CC BY-NC-ND. The actual licence must be checked per DIKSHA resource ID before reuse; platform-wide acceptance of licence types does not establish the licence of a particular item.
- Biological specimen identification requires expert-reviewed taxonomy and diagnostic traits. Unknown identity returns NEEDS_REVIEW.
- Temporary slide preparation and animal-tissue work must be virtual-only in LabVerse until teacher-reviewed protocols, safety and disposal controls are specified.
- Do not infer pathogen handling or culture instructions from an index title. Bacteria/fungi learning should use reviewed images/models or non-culturing virtual representations unless a separately approved safe method exists.
