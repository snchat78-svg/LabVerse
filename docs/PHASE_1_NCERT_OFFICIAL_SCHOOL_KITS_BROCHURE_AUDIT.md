# Phase 1 — NCERT Official School Kits Brochure Audit

Audit date: 2026-11-01  
Status: OFFICIAL HIGH-LEVEL BIOLOGY KIT DESCRIPTION INSPECTED / ITEM-LEVEL SPECIFICATIONS STILL OPEN

## 1. Primary source inspected

NCERT, *NCERT School Kits*, Division of Educational Kits.  
Canonical PDF: https://www.ncert.nic.in/division/dek/pdf/NCERT_Educational_School_Kit-Enhancing-UN278.pdf

The PDF text and the relevant page image (printed page 8; PDF page index 9) were inspected. The retrieved document text does not expose a clear publication/reprint date or ISBN; do not infer a release year from the search-index crawl date.

## 2. Source-supported Biology kit facts

On printed page 8, NCERT identifies a “Higher Secondary Biology Lab. Kit (HSBLK)” and states that it is intended to contain items/equipment for experiments mentioned in Biology syllabi of Classes XI and XII. The same paragraph identifies broad item families:
- laboratory instruments;
- glassware;
- compound microscope;
- permanent slides;
- stains;
- cards.

This is official, high-level confirmation that the kit is intended for the higher-secondary Biology syllabus and that these broad families are in scope.

## 3. What this source does not establish

The brochure is not an item-level bill of materials. It does not confirm:
- exact item names, number of pieces or quantities;
- dimensions, materials, grades, tolerances or operating ranges;
- acceptance tests or replacement/consumable rules;
- current procurement revision;
- which items are included in the microscope and no-microscope variants;
- whether every pictured object is supplied in each variant.

The brochure's product photograph is contextual only. No item IDs are inferred from the photo.

## 4. Reconciliation with the current NCERT indexes

The official NCERT kit-manual index lists a Higher Secondary Biology Laboratory Kit manual for Grades 11 and 12. The official specification index lists two separate variants: Biology kit with microscope and Biology kit without microscope. Keep microscope inclusion as a variant-dependent property; do not mark it universally included.

The brochure's general HSBLK paragraph and the index listings support the kit family and broad item families, but they do not replace retrieval and inspection of the full Biology kit manual or either specification attachment.

## 5. LabVerse use and rights boundary

- Use the brochure only as source metadata and broad category evidence.
- Keep each concrete apparatus/slide/reagent record linked to an inspectable item-level source before marking it VERIFIED.
- Do not copy the brochure's photograph, manual diagrams, text or artwork into LabVerse assets without permission. Create original explanatory text and 3D assets or obtain appropriate rights.
- Hazardous reagent and biological handling rules remain bounded by the safety policy; the brochure does not validate any procedure.
