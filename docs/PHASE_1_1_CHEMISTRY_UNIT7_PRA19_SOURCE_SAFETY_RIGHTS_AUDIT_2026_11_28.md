## 3. High-consequence safety boundary

- The source describes sodium-fusion/Lassaigne chemistry. Its own precautions identify highly reactive sodium and the possibility of vigorous or violent reaction where moisture or residual sodium is involved. This audit deliberately omits operational fusion, ignition, quench and extraction instructions.
- The p.116 warning expressly excludes chloroform and carbon tetrachloride as **fusion samples** because violent explosions may result. This is distinct from the later p.119 layer-test reference to carbon tetrachloride/carbon disulphide; that separate reference is not treated as solvent approval or a safe alternative.
- The source identifies cyanide-, sulphide- and thiocyanate-containing chemistry and mentions HCN/H2S in the combined-element branch. No cyanide/sulphide reaction model, gas-generation action, observation or result is implemented.
- The p.119 hazard panel warns of a serious explosion risk involving silver-halide precipitates dissolved in ammonium hydroxide and later acidified with nitric acid. No acidification, neutralisation or waste-disposal workflow is authorised.
- The source's own precautions are not a modern risk assessment. Identity-specific SDS, organic-sample identity, exposure/engineering controls, compatibility, waste route and qualified Chemistry/lab-safety review are absent.

**Blocked:** sodium fusion, heating/ignition, water quench, gas generation, halogen solvent extraction, silver/ammonia/acid waste handling and all live-use controls. A safer curriculum-aligned alternative must be identified and reviewed before any learner-facing simulation is considered.
