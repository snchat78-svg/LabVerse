## 2. Exercise 2 — representative bacteria, fungi and plant groups

Current manual identity: Exercise 2, printed p.8.  
Legacy body state: text inspected; initial page screenshot inspected.

The chapter uses microscope slides, hand-sections, photographs and preserved/fresh specimens for representative bacteria, cyanobacterial/algal forms, fungi, lichens, bryophytes, pteridophytes, gymnosperms and angiosperms. Examples named in the legacy source include Oscillatoria, Spirogyra, Rhizopus, yeast, mushroom, lichen growth forms, Marchantia, Funaria, Dryopteris, Pinus and monocot/dicot representatives such as maize and pea/sunflower.

Candidate asset families:
- BIO-SLIDE-BACTERIA-REP and BIO-SLIDE-OSCILLATORIA
- BIO-SLIDE-SPIROGYRA, BIO-SLIDE-RHIZOPUS and BIO-SLIDE-YEAST
- BIO-SPECIMEN-AGARICUS and BIO-SPECIMEN-LICHEN-FORMS
- BIO-SPECIMEN-MARCHANTIA, BIO-SPECIMEN-FUNARIA and BIO-SPECIMEN-DRYOPTERIS
- BIO-SPECIMEN-PINUS
- BIO-SPECIMEN-ANGIOSPERM-MONOCOT and BIO-SPECIMEN-ANGIOSPERM-DICOT

Interaction should focus on zooming prepared slides, rotating specimens, viewing source-supported structures and comparing representative forms. Do not make microbial/fungal culturing or spore-handling a LabVerse learner workflow. Use expert-reviewed prepared slides, licensed images or original virtual models. The legacy source warns against inhaling fungal spores.

Do not silently modernise the source taxonomy. Preserve the original source identity and add any accepted modern names only through a separately cited taxonomy source and reviewer.

## 4. Exercise 4 — plant tissues and cell-shape diversity
