## 2. Exercise 4 — gametogenesis in mammalian testis and ovary

The source describes permanent transverse-section slides of testis and ovary and a compound microscope.

Source-backed content includes:
- Testis: seminiferous tubules and development sequence from germinal epithelium/spermatogonial cells through primary and secondary spermatocytes, spermatids and spermatozoa; interstitial/Leydig cells are also identified.
- Ovary: ovum with surrounding follicular cell layers; follicular development including antrum, Graafian follicle, corpus luteum and corpus albicans.
- Observation uses lower and higher magnification and labelled diagrams.

Candidate IDs (DESIGN_ONLY):
- BIO-XII-SLIDE-TESTIS-TS
- BIO-XII-SLIDE-OVARY-TS
- BIO-XII-GAMETOGENESIS-SPERMATOGENESIS-STAGES
- BIO-XII-GAMETOGENESIS-OOGENESIS-STAGES
- BIO-XII-SLIDE-CLEANING-KIT

Safety/ethics: use licensed/pre-prepared histology images or original virtual slide representations; no collection, dissection or handling of human/animal reproductive tissue. Avoid diagnostic or fertility-related interpretation; the task is morphology/learning only.

## 3. Exercise 5 — female gametophyte development

The source describes permanent vertical-section slides of ovary and photographs/charts/models showing the developmental stages of the female gametophyte.

Source-backed content includes:
- Ovules within the ovary and their integuments, nucellus, micropyle and chalazal pole;
- embryo sac development, including a common 8-nucleate, 7-celled form;
- egg apparatus at the micropylar end, central/secondary nucleus and antipodal cells at the chalazal end.

Candidate IDs (DESIGN_ONLY):
- BIO-XII-SLIDE-OVULE-VS
- BIO-XII-FEMALE-GAMETOPHYTE-STAGE-MEGASPORE
- BIO-XII-FEMALE-GAMETOPHYTE-STAGE-2NUCLEATE
- BIO-XII-FEMALE-GAMETOPHYTE-STAGE-4NUCLEATE
- BIO-XII-FEMALE-GAMETOPHYTE-STAGE-8NUCLEATE
- BIO-XII-OVULE-STRUCTURE-MODEL

Model requirements: microscope zoom/focus, slide orientation, labelled ovule anatomy, developmental stage comparison and uncertainty/visibility states. Do not claim a single species-specific embryo-sac development pattern unless a source/species is explicitly attached to the model.

## 4. Exercise 6 — onion root-tip mitosis

The source explicitly names preparation and study of mitosis in onion root tips. It describes root-tip growth from onion bulbs, fixed root material, staining, slide preparation and compound-microscope observation.

Source-backed observations:
- Onion roots may take several days to grow before root-tip sampling.
- The source uses chemical fixative/staining and an acid treatment, with heat during slide preparation.
- Initial search uses a 10X objective; higher magnification is used for detail.
- Cell stages include interphase, prophase, metaphase, anaphase and telophase.
- Cytokinesis includes plant-cell plate formation; the text also contrasts animal-cell constriction.
- Mitotic index is calculated as dividing cells divided by total cells in a selected microscopic field, multiplied by 100.

Candidate IDs (DESIGN_ONLY):
- BIO-XII-SPECIMEN-ONION-ROOT-TIP-VIRTUAL
- BIO-XII-SLIDE-ONION-ROOT-MITOSIS
- BIO-XII-CELL-STAGE-INTERPHASE
- BIO-XII-CELL-STAGE-PROPHASE
- BIO-XII-CELL-STAGE-METAPHASE
- BIO-XII-CELL-STAGE-ANAPHASE
- BIO-XII-CELL-STAGE-TELOPHASE
- BIO-XII-MITOTIC-INDEX-COUNTER

Critical safety boundary: the source lists glacial acetic acid, ethanol, hydrochloric acid, acetocarmine/acetoorcein stain and heating equipment. LabVerse must not convert this into a home experiment or real reagent-preparation workflow. The learner experience should use a pre-prepared/synthetic virtual slide, a modelled growth timeline and safe count/analysis controls only. No heating, acid, solvent, fixative or biological collection instructions are to be exposed.
