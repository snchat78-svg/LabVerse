## 4. Cross-cutting limitations

1. Some activities use consumables or instruments not established in the Physics manual list: paper, wax, detergent, insulating samples, jet nozzle/reservoir, pivot hardware and deflection gauge.
2. Activity names alone do not establish a unique apparatus configuration. Preserve the missing geometry and variables as UNKNOWN.
3. Water-jet trajectory requires a clear distinction between constant-speed projectile launch and an outlet jet whose speed depends on water head/nozzle conditions.
4. Rolling friction must not be silently treated as sliding Coulomb friction.
5. Molten wax and all heating activities are virtual-only; no real flame, hot-liquid or burn instructions are exposed.
6. Heat-loss and beam-deflection experiments require controlled conditions and correct boundary conditions before a model can emit quantitative results.
7. NCERT Physics manual copyright remains applicable; LabVerse should create original 3D geometry/text and obtain rights review.
