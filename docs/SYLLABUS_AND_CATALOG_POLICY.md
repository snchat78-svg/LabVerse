# Curriculum and Scientific Catalogue Policy

## Product scope

LabVerse is not limited to school practicals. RBSE/NCERT Class 11–12 Physics, Chemistry and Biology are the first curriculum pack and a testbed for the wider virtual experiment and scientific analysis system. Additional boards, education levels, advanced topics and research packs are separate versioned collections.

## Curriculum authority order

1. Current official RBSE syllabus/practical instructions for the selected examination session are primary for the Rajasthan board pack.
2. Official NCERT Class XI/XII laboratory manuals and kit manuals support procedure, apparatus and scientific context.
3. CBSE curriculum may be a separately tagged comparison/compatibility source; it must not override RBSE.
4. Secondary websites may help locate a source but are not final evidence for a syllabus claim.

## Catalogue row requirements

Every practical/activity/project row includes stable ID/title; board/class/subject/session; exact official document/page/section/URL; category; objective/prerequisites; apparatus/instruments/glassware/chemicals/specimens; procedure and allowed simulated actions; observations/outcomes; equations/calculations/units; 3D asset and interaction requirements; safety/supervision tags; author/reviewer/verification date; status/version.

## Broader scientific catalogue

Beyond curriculum items, research and simulation records may cover:
- Physics models/equations/constants/material properties and measurement standards.
- Chemistry substances, properties, spectra, reactions and thermochemical data.
- Biology taxonomy, organisms, genomes/proteins, cellular structures, ecological observations and experimental methods.
- Cross-domain topics such as biophysics, environmental science, materials science and chemical biology.

Every record must disclose data origin, source quality, licence/terms, version/date, units, applicability and known limitations. Do not copy all content from a third-party database merely because its search page is public.

## Completeness process

1. Record official documents by class, subject, board, session, title, publication date, retrieval date and checksum where possible.
2. Extract practicals, activities, projects, investigatory work, spotting, specimen/slide tasks, records and viva.
3. Map each item to apparatus/material IDs, simulation behaviours, observations and tests.
4. Compare coverage against every official source heading and listed practical.
5. Mark ambiguity, mismatch or missing evidence NEEDS_REVIEW.
6. Have a qualified teacher/subject reviewer check content before VERIFIED.
7. Repeat audit when an official board issues a new session.

Do not claim all practicals are covered until the latest official documents are reviewed and the coverage matrix has no unexplained gaps.
