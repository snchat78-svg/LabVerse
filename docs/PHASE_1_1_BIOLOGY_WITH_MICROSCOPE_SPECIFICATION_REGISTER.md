## Source transcription and review boundary

- Rows 1–83: OCR-derived from specification pages; selected entries were visually checked, including row 46 Square Container Box and row 83 Dissecting Microscope.
- Row 84: Amoeba visual card specification visually inspected: 300 gsm plastic-coated art paper, 210 x 300 mm, Hindi/English (1+1), quantity 1. The other 49 visual-card names are indexed but their individual specification pages were not all OCR-verified in this pass.
- Row 134: Utricularia embedded specimen visually inspected: acrylic-embedded original specimen, 85 x 60 x 14 mm, 1 piece. Other six specimens remain index-only here.
- Row 141: W.M. Spirillum permanent slide visually inspected: borosilicate glass, 75 x 25 x 1 mm, contrast-stained whole mount, organism information label, 1 piece. Other 34 slide pages remain index-only here.
- Row 176: microscope visually inspected. The index heading says “Permanent Slide: Student Laboratory Microscope”, but the specification text and photograph identify a physical student microscope. Preserve the source heading in provenance and use “Student Laboratory Microscope” as the canonical LabVerse item label.

## Significant technical and safety flags

- Row 79 specifies 100 x 100 mm stainless-steel wire gauze with an asbestos centre diameter 55 mm. Do not create an asbestos-use asset; flag this legacy specification and require a separately reviewed asbestos-free virtual substitute.
- Row 83 is a Dissecting Microscope with a plastic body and rack-and-pinion, 10X/20X eyepieces, 80 x 80 mm platform, 50 mm mirror and 110 mm platform height. The source phrase for its base/rack dimensions is awkward (“120 x 15/18 mm”); preserve it and request technical review.
- Row 176 is a distinct student laboratory microscope: metal body, 10X/45X objectives, 10X/15X eyepieces with pointer, movable slide clip, coarse/fine adjustments, fixed condenser and ISI mark to IS 3686.
- Sharp tools, heating, broken glass, hammer/nails and field equipment remain virtual-only or teacher-controlled.
