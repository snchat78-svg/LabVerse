## 4. Research-specific integrity rules

1. Water-quality results require sample collection date/location, preservation, laboratory method, detection limit, calibration and source dataset provenance. Do not infer contamination cause from a single sulphide result.
2. Purification comparisons are analyte-specific; clear appearance does not prove safe drinking water.
3. Regulatory comparisons require a named standard, jurisdiction and effective/revision date.
4. Soap foaming, tea acidity/colour, evaporation and fibre tensile comparisons require controlled protocols and replicates.
5. Food acidity and mineral composition are different measurements. pH is not a proxy for mineral content.
6. Data imported from public sources must retain licence, attribution, retrieval date, transformation history and uncertainty.
7. Unknown, proprietary or hazardous mixtures remain NOT_MODELLED/NEEDS_REVIEW; no arbitrary reagent mixing or operational corrosive/volatile chemical instructions.
8. The MSCL source copy includes mercury/asbestos hazards; neither is a learner-usable asset.
