## 3. Proposed scientific model scopes and test vectors

### RBSE-XI-PHY-PRJ-01 — Investigate conservation of energy of a simple pendulum

**Proposed model:** PHY-OSCILLATION-PENDULUM-ENERGY-001. For small oscillations, track mechanical energy as gravitational potential + translational kinetic (and rotational kinetic if the bob is not a point mass); state damping and pivot-loss assumptions.

**Planned tests (NOT RUN):** TV-XI-PHY-PRJ-01-ENERGY-CYCLE; TV-XI-PHY-PRJ-01-UNDAMPED; TV-XI-PHY-PRJ-01-DAMPED-LOSS; TV-XI-PHY-PRJ-01-UNIT.

**Safety:** Virtual oscillation only; label simulated energy separately from measured data.

**Review:** Candidate manual item presence is confirmed; exact project-to-item equivalence, apparatus specification and reviewer approval remain open. All projects need qualified Physics teacher review, source-rights review and model validation before publication.

### RBSE-XI-PHY-PRJ-02 — Determine radius of gyration of a metre scale used as a bar pendulum

**Proposed model:** PHY-ROTATION-BAR-PENDULUM-RADIUS-GYRATION-001. For a physical pendulum, T = 2π√(I/(mgh)); radius of gyration about the pivot is √(I/m), and parallel-axis conversion is needed for the centre-of-mass radius.

**Planned tests (NOT RUN):** TV-XI-PHY-PRJ-02-PIVOT-DISTANCE; TV-XI-PHY-PRJ-02-PERIOD; TV-XI-PHY-PRJ-02-PARALLEL-AXIS; TV-XI-PHY-PRJ-02-UNCERTAINTY.

**Safety:** Virtual pendulum only; unsupported pivot geometry returns NEEDS_REVIEW.

**Review:** Candidate manual item presence is confirmed; exact project-to-item equivalence, apparatus specification and reviewer approval remain open. All projects need qualified Physics teacher review, source-rights review and model validation before publication.
