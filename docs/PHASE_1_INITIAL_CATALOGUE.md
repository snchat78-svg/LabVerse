## Class 11 — Physics

The 2026 baseline includes practical work such as:
1. Vernier calipers: small object diameter, dimensions/density and internal diameter/depth/volume.
2. Screw gauge: wire diameter, sheet thickness and irregular sheet volume.
3. Spherometer: radius of curvature of a spherical surface.
4. Beam balance: compare masses.
5. Parallelogram law of vector addition and weight/force.
6. Simple pendulum: L–T and L–T² graphs and effective length.
7. Limiting friction and coefficient of friction.
8. Inclined plane: force versus sin(theta).
9. Young's modulus of a wire.
10. Spring oscillations: T²–mass graph, spring constant and effective mass.
11. Boyle's law: P–V and P–1/V graphs.
12. Surface tension by capillary rise.
13. Viscosity by terminal velocity of a sphere in a liquid.
14. Cooling curve and temperature-time relation.
15. Sonometer: string length, frequency and tension relationships.

The baseline document asks students to maintain records for at least 12 experiments and 8 activities. The exact current requirement must be checked against the 2027 document.

## Class 11 — Chemistry

The 2026 baseline is structured into:
- laboratory introduction, discipline and safety;
- basic laboratory techniques: glass tube/rod operations, heating, filtration, liquid-volume measurement, weighing and standard solution preparation;
- purification by crystallisation and purity by melting/boiling point;
- ionic equilibrium experiments involving ferric/thiocyanate and cobalt/chloride systems;
- pH of fruit juices, dilution effects, common-ion effect and salt solutions;
- acidimetry/alkalimetry and standard-solution titration;
- systematic qualitative analysis;
- detection of nitrogen, sulphur and halogens in organic compounds;
- projects, practical record and viva.

Material inventory candidates include test tubes, beakers, flasks, glass rods/tubes, corks, filtration apparatus, measuring/volumetric equipment, balance, heating apparatus, pH indicators/meters and syllabus-approved reagents. Final inventory must be built from exact practical steps and kit manuals.

## Class 11 — Biology

The 2026 baseline includes:
- local flowering-plant family identification and description;
- transverse-section slides of dicot/monocot roots and stems;
- stomatal distribution on upper/lower leaf surfaces;
- osmosis with a potato osmometer;
- plant-pigment separation by paper chromatography;
- plasmolysis in epidermal peels;
- respiration in flower buds/germinating seeds;
- transpiration comparison between leaf surfaces;
- salivary amylase effect on starch;
- sugar, starch, protein and fat tests;
- urine tests for sugar/albumin and urea/bile salts;
- root/stem/leaf morphology and spotting of specimens, including microscope work.

Botany/zoology major/minor, slide and spotting sections need full line-by-line extraction and an asset inventory.

## Class 12 — Physics

The 2026 baseline includes:
- resistance/resistivity using V-I observations and graphs;
- metre bridge and resistance combinations;
- potentiometer comparison of cell EMF and internal resistance;
- galvanometer resistance/figure of merit and conversion to ammeter/voltmeter;
- AC frequency using sonometer/electromagnet;
- concave mirror and convex/concave lens focal lengths;
- prism minimum deviation/refractive index;
- refractive index of glass slab/liquid;
- p-n junction I-V characteristics;
- Zener diode reverse-breakdown characteristic.

The baseline requires practical record work for at least 12 experiments and 8 activities; verify for the current session.

## Class 12 — Chemistry

The 2026 baseline includes:
- redox titration: determine KMnO4 concentration/molarity using standard oxalic acid or ferrous ammonium sulphate;
- qualitative analysis: identify one cation and one anion from the specified syllabus list;
- organic functional-group tests (including unsaturation, alcohol, phenol, aldehyde, ketone, carboxyl and primary amino groups);
- alternative preparation of organic/inorganic compounds or food tests for carbohydrates, fats and proteins;
- content-based experiment, practical record and viva.

Inventory candidates include burette, pipette, conical flask, measuring cylinder, volumetric flask, balance, indicators, approved standard solutions and specific syllabus reagents. Hazard metadata and rule-level validation are mandatory.

## Class 12 — Biology

The 2026 baseline includes:
- botany major practicals such as reproductive structures of common flowers, onion root-tip mitosis slide, quadrat-based plant density/frequency, female gametophyte stages and nucleic-acid staining;
- zoology major/minor practical categories, specimen and spotting work;
- pollen germination percentage, pollen-tube development, meiosis slides, Mendel's laws, homologous/analogous organs, blastula stage and pedigree analysis;
- investigatory project, record and viva.

**Source wording review flag:** the 2026 document's section headed “Zoology Major Exercise” includes soil texture, water-holding capacity, water/soil pH, water organisms, turbidity and air suspended particulate matter. The catalogue will preserve the official source wording and flag the heading/content mismatch for teacher review, rather than silently reclassifying it.
